Nepal, Rasuwa district: a glacial lake outburst flood, 26 August. Copernicus EMS graded 3,207 buildings from 50cm imagery. 2,521 destroyed. 79%.

A dam and two power stations gone. 47km of road cut.

Copernicus publishes what was damaged. Not what it cost. That's the gap this model fills: 36 to 39 million euros modelled loss, priced from the graded damage itself, not an assumed water depth.
